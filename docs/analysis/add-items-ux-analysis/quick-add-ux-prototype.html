Quick add: a title typed where the item will go

Design E of the add items frames, as a working control. Each horizon and section ends with a quiet “Add an item” (“Add the first item” where it has none). Choosing it opens a title field in its place. Enter, or Add, adds the item there, title only, and opens a fresh field for the next. Escape, Cancel, or leaving the field any other way, drops what is typed and puts “Add an item” back. Add and Cancel show on every screen, since a page cannot tell whether a keyboard with an Escape key is attached: under the field in the cards view and on a phone, beside it in the compact view on a wider screen. The expand button at the end of the field, “Add more details”, opens the item dialog with the title typed so far; Cancel there brings the field back as it was. Try it in the cards view and the compact view. Remove on a new card drops it at once here; the app asks first (STY-049). Move, Edit, a published card's Remove, Publish and Discard are drawn but do nothing. In the compact view, as in the app, Remove is at the foot of an opened row, which this page does not draw. Move and Edit are 32px tall, as in the app; a form's buttons are 40px.

Things to try
  1. Choose “Add an item” under Next, type “Book a hearing test online” and press Enter. Type “Change your appointment by text” and press Enter again. Then press Escape.
  2. Type a title, then click somewhere else, or press Tab: nothing is added and “Add an item” comes back.
  3. While typing under Next, choose “Add an item” under Later: Next's title is dropped and Later's field opens.
  4. Press Enter with nothing typed, or only spaces.
  5. Add “ see your waiting time ”, which Next already has, in other words with extra spaces.
  6. Remove a new item from its card.
  7. On Phone 375, add two items with the Add button, then Cancel. Type a title and Tab onto Add: the field stays open.
  8. Type “Book a hearing test”, choose the expand button at the end of the field, write a description and choose “Add item”. Do it again and choose Cancel, or press Escape: the field comes back with the title in it.
  9. Switch to Compact and do the same.
  10. Look at Not doing, which has no items: it offers “Add the first item”. Add one there.
  11. Do it all with the keyboard alone, on the phone, and in the dark theme.
