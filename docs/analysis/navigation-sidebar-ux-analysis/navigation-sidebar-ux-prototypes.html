The navigation: one working design to try

The shell's navigation, working, beside a page of the app. It adds a search at the top that narrows the roadmaps as you type, with the fold button on the same row; families that open and close from their name; an edge you drag to set its width on a desktop; and, while more than one roadmap has unpublished changes, a count you press to show just those roadmaps.

Things to try in each
  1. Drag the navigation's right edge to make it wider, then narrower. It stops at 208px and at 400px. Double-click the edge to put it back to 256px.
  2. Close "Single Record" with the arrow at the end of its name. It keeps a dot, since Shared care plans inside it has unpublished changes. Open it again.
  3. Type "prescri" in the search: a roadmap from each of two families. Then try "care", which finds Carers' register as well as the two care roadmaps, and "ffon", which finds Ffôn Iechyd helpline without its accent.
  4. Type "single": a family's name finds all its roadmaps. Type "xyz" to see nothing found, then press Escape or the cross to clear the search.
  5. Press "Close all" above the families: every family closes, and the button becomes "Open all". Open Single Record alone with its arrow: the button says "Close all" again. Press it, then "Open all".
  6. Close "Single Record", then search "shared". The closed family's roadmap is found. Clear the search: the family is still closed.
  7. Type "care", then press "3 roadmaps have unpublished changes". The search clears and only those three show. Press it again: every roadmap shows, and "care" does not come back.
  8. Press the count, then start typing in the search: the count lets go, and the search takes over.
  9. With the count pressed, open Electronic prescribing and press Discard changes: two are left, still shown alone. Discard another: with one left, the count goes back to leading to it.
  10. Set Unpublished changes to "One roadmap": the count leads to that roadmap, as it does today, and does not narrow the list.
  11. Press the fold button. The search becomes a spyglass, and the rail scrolls without a scrollbar. Press the spyglass, and the navigation opens with the search ready to type in. Fold again and press the count's circle.
  12. Choose Phone 375 and press Menu. The search and Close menu share the top row. Press the count: the drawer stays open. Choose a roadmap: it closes.
  13. On Laptop 1024, drag the navigation to its widest, and see how much room the page keeps.
  14. Do it all with the keyboard alone. The edge is the navigation's last stop: press the left and right arrows, Home and End. Then try the dark theme.
