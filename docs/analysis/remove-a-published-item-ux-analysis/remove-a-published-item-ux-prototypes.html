STY-049 — Removing a published item: UX prototypes

Three ways a Product Lead removes an item the public site already shows, then three ways a removal is published when a co-lead has changed the item meanwhile. Sam Evans removes “Fax referrals” from the published Electronic Referrals roadmap, with one change already pending (Referral status for patients: Phase). Static frames: desktop then phone for each moment. Analysis: docs/analysis/remove-a-published-item-ux-analysis.md.

1. Removing an item — Q1 pending or at once, Q3 Not doing

A1 inline: Remove on the card; the card stays where it was, struck through, until published (Q1a; no Not doing mention, Q3b).

A2 guided: Remove asks first, offering Not doing before removal; removal is written at once (Q1b, Q3a).

A3 mode: Remove from the roadmap is the last place in the Move menu, under Not doing; the card waits in its own group at the foot of the page until published (Q1a, Q3a without a confirmation).

2. Publishing a removal — Q2 a co-lead's change

Alex Jones changed the Description of “Fax referrals” after Sam opened the roadmap.

B1: the publish dialog lists the removal, and Alex's change stops the publish as a conflict, decided in the existing review (Q2a).

B2: the removal wins; the published notice says Alex's change went with it (Q2b).

B3: with removal at once (A2), the same conflict is decided inside the question (Q1b with Q2a).
