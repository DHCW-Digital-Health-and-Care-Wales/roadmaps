Seeing where a moved item went: four designs, and the one chosen

Each design is the items area of a roadmap's page, in a window as tall as the room a screen gives it. Every Move menu works: move an item up, down, to the top or bottom of its placement, or to another placement, and watch what tells you where it went. Every design scrolls the moved item into view and puts focus on its Move, and says what the move came to, as the app does for a move within a placement; a move to another placement adds a line. They differ in what a sighted Product Lead sees.

A fades a highlight after a moment; B marks the item, in words, until the next move; C shows the items travelling to their new places; D leaves a trace where the item was. E is the owner's choice of 2026-11-19: C's slide, then B's mark.

Things to try in each
  1. In Now, move “Text reminders before every appointment” Down one. Its neighbour, “Text reminders before every video appointment”, reads almost the same: can you tell, at a glance, which one moved?
  2. Move “Check in from your phone on arrival” To the top, then straight back To the bottom: two moves close together.
  3. Move “See your place on the waiting list” to Later. It lands last there, out of sight: does the design take you to it, and can you find your way back?
  4. Switch to the Compact view and do the same three.
  5. After a move, look away for five seconds, then look back: is it still clear which item moved?
  6. Do it all by keyboard alone: Tab to a Move, Enter, the arrow keys, Enter.
  7. Try it on the phone, with Motion set to Reduced, and in the dark theme.
  8. Choose “Undo order” beside Now’s heading after a move.
