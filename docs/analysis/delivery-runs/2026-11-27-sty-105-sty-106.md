## Neon assessment

### Method and limits

The delivery agent's reviewer records were written by Neon grading itself and its peers, so this assessment re-read the reviewers' own final reports and checked the records against them and the diffs. Checked: every finding graded P2 or 🔴 on either PR, every finding only one lane raised that counts for or against Neon, both PRs' declined findings, and a sample of the rest: about 40 of the 67 distinct findings on the two primary PRs. Three claims were checked in git: when the two STY-106 final-pass P2s first appeared, and one factual charge in the STY-105 record. The retro PRs were graded by Sonnet retro agents, not by Neon; their records were read but not re-checked line by line. Whether each fix is correct was not re-verified, and no test was rerun for this record.

Confounders: only two items, both Python API stories in one epic; on the primary PRs Neon reviewed code Neon wrote; lanes were dispatched at slightly different moments within each batch; Opus ran fewer passes by rule (no final passes); tokens and cost are unknown for every lane; and no Opus or Sonnet delivery ran in this run, so there is no delivery baseline.

### Assessment

- **Neon correctness: a useful extra pair of eyes, not yet shown to beat the existing pair.** Over two items it raised as many sole accepted findings as `gpt-6.1-sol` and Opus together (three against three) but only one high one against `gpt-6.1-sol`'s two, and it missed five P2s other lanes found at heads it passed. Its final passes were its best work, as in the earlier trial. It costs about 6 min a task against 2 for `gpt-6.1-sol`. Confidence low.
- **Neon quality: not a substitute for Sonnet, and its clean verdicts are unreliable.** It matched Sonnet on #346 (15 against 14 accepted, 4 sole each) and raised one 🔴 Sonnet missed, but raised half as much on #344, no 🔴 there, and passed both final heads where Sonnet still found issues, a 🔴 among them. About 2.3 times Sonnet's median time. Confidence low to moderate.
- **Neon delivery: capable, with weaker process discipline and self-reporting.** It delivered two security-relevant stories through every review round without intervention, and #344 merged first time. Against that: five fix regressions, three process misses on #346, and records that needed four corrections. No same-run baseline exists, so this does not rank it against Opus or Sonnet delivery.
- **Cost beyond time is unknown.** No lane reported tokens or price.

Compared with the [2026-11-24 trial](2026-11-24-epc-022-neon-review-trial.md): that run had Claude delivery agents grading Neon's reviews over eight PRs. The correctness-lane finding repeats (useful final passes, slower), and Neon quality again raised fewer findings than Sonnet overall, though this time it matched Sonnet on one PR.

## Learnings and next evaluation

- What helped or caused avoidable cost: each of the six lanes found something no other did. Fable bounded review wall clock on the primary PRs, and Neon on the retros. Fix commits caused five regressions, and the re-checks and final passes caught each. Both final-pass P2s on #346 had been in the code since round 1. A delivery agent grading its own lanes made errors in both directions, so independent checking was needed.
- Orchestration retro: no-op. The 15-minute fallback wake fired about 17 times over both sessions with no stall; earlier records already weighed that cost against the missed notification it guards and kept it. The other frictions happened once: a hand-back written before the task ended, and a misstatement about which lanes a retro would run. Re-check briefs in the STY-105 retro listed other lanes' fixes, which the delegation reference already forbids. #345 and #347 own the item-level changes.
- Follow-up work: CHO-055 (in #345). The process misses on #346 (unread parts, post-cap declines, sleep-based tests) sit with that open PR and its owner review.
- Next comparison: if Neon stays as a correctness lane, compare its sole high findings and misses with `gpt-6.1-sol`'s over about ten PRs, delivered by a non-Neon agent and graded by someone other than the delivery agent, with token or price data if the runtime reports it. No policy change is made by this record.
