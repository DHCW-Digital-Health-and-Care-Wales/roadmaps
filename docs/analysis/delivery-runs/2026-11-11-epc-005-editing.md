## Learnings and next evaluation

- What helped:
  - The hand-back check caught both child slips before a merge: the child's head against
    the PR's, and distinct reviewer task IDs against the claimed passes.
    - STY-022's PR was raised before its reviews.
    - STY-025's head was unpushed.
  - Per-item orchestrator threads kept each session small.
  - Merging between items kept every dependant on a fresh `main`.
  - Every PR's CI was green first time.
- Avoidable cost:
  - The first dispatch went to a provider that was rate-limited, which cost 26 min.
  - Children's progress-log stage names drifted from the shared brief on several items.
    This cost nothing beyond mapping the names for this record.
  - Neither was changed: one was a provider limit, the other adherence to guidance that is
    already clear.
- Follow-up work:
  - No new backlog record.
  - The owner adds an `Item ID` column to the live `Template` tab and the public site's
    README (ADR-012's format change).
  - Fluent Welsh review is tracked in the README's Welsh note.
  - [DEF-002](../../backlog/defects/DEF-002-header-home-link-name-on-phones.md) is still
    open.
- Missed: #32 merged while the Codex review was still running, and its 2 P2s arrived
  after the merge (DEF-003). A merge should wait for a running bot review to finish.
- Next comparison: on Opus stories with concurrency or cache logic (STY-025, STY-026), the
  final fresh pass found new P2s that were fixed after the cap without a re-check. One of
  STY-026's escapes landed in such a fix. Watch for another such escape before changing
  the review cap.
